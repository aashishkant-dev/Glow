08 — Usage

Keep it clean.

Clear space & minimum size
Keep padding of at least half the mark's height on every side. Never place the mark below 24 px in UI or 16 px as a favicon.

Do

  • Use flat solid fills — green mark on light, white mark on dark.
  • Drive the whole mark from one color prop.
  • Give it generous clear space and test it at 24 px.

Don't

  • Add gradients, shadows, filters or url(#…) — they render blank in React Native.
  • Recolor or outline the inner heart, cross or shield.
  • Stretch the pin or set it on a busy background.

09 — Raw SVG
